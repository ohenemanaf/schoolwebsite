Admissions

Applications are open for the 2027/2028 academic year. Here is how it works.

Step by step

Five steps to your child’s first day

  1. 1

    Send an enquiry

    Use the form on this page, call, or visit the school office.

  2. 2

    Bring the documents

    Birth certificate, last school report, two passport photos and a parent’s ID.

  3. 3

    Placement assessment

    New Primary and JHS pupils do a short English and Mathematics assessment.

  4. 4

    Meet the Headmaster

    A short conversation with parents. We give our answer within 5 working days.

  5. 5

    Pay and start

    Pay the first term’s fees and collect the uniform list, timetable and welcome pack.

The application form costs GH₵ 30, paid at the school office.

What we look for

Before you apply

Age

Nursery pupils should be at least 2, and Kindergarten pupils 4, by September.

Readiness

For Primary and JHS we check reading and numbers so each child is placed in the right class.

Shared values

We welcome families who share our commitment to discipline, respect and hard work.

Questions

Parents often ask

Something else? Call 030 000 0000.

When can my child join?

Most pupils join in September, at the start of Term 1. We also admit pupils in Terms 2 and 3 when there are places.

What time does school start and end?

Pupils should arrive by 7:30 am for assembly at 8:00 am. Classes end at 2:30 pm for Creche and Kindergarten and 3:00 pm for Primary and JHS.

Do you offer discounts for siblings?

Yes. The second child gets 5% off, the third 10% and the fourth or later 15%. See the fees page for details.

Do you provide meals?

Creche and Kindergarten pupils get a mid-morning snack. Lunch can be bought at the school canteen, or pupils can bring their own.

How do parents hear about school news?

Through notices on this website, termly PTA meetings, and messages from class teachers.

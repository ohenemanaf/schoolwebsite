About Greenfield Academy

Building a community of learners, leaders and caring citizens since 1994.

A welcome note

From our Headmaster

At Greenfield Academy, education is more than results. It is about building the character and resilience children need to thrive in a fast-changing world.

For over three decades we have kept the same promise: a safe, caring school where every pupil is known by name, valued and challenged to reach their best. Welcome to our family.

What guides us

Mission, vision and values

Our story

32 years of growing together

  1. 1994

    The first classrooms

    Greenfield opened with 3 classrooms, 8 teachers and 80 pupils, to bring good primary education to the community.

  2. 2005

    Junior High School

    At parents’ request we opened the JHS block, with the school’s first computer room and science corner.

  3. 2015

    Creche and new library

    A purpose-built creche and a library of over 15,000 books made Greenfield a school from the very first years.

  4. 2023

    Best Community School

    Named Best Community School, and opened a new sports field and digital resource centre.

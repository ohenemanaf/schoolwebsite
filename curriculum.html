Academics

The national standards-based curriculum, taught with care from Creche to Junior High School.

How we teach

Learning with purpose

We follow the national curriculum and focus on understanding and practical skills, not memorising.

Thinking, not memorising

Pupils learn to question, test ideas and solve real problems.

Digital skills

Computing is part of learning from Upper Primary onwards.

Speaking and teamwork

Group projects, debates and presentations build confidence.

Levels

What your child will learn

Creche and Nursery

Ages 1 to 3

A calm, safe start focused on health, wellbeing and early development, through play, songs and stories.

Subjects

  • Play and movement
  • Songs and rhymes
  • Early language
  • Social skills

Kindergarten

KG 1 and KG 2, ages 4 to 5

Play-based learning built on the 4Rs: Reading, wRiting, aRithmetic and cReativity.

Subjects

  • Literacy
  • Numeracy
  • Creative Arts
  • Our World, Our People

Primary School

Basic 1 to Basic 6

The national standards-based curriculum, with Computing and French from Basic 4 and the National Standard Assessment Test (NSAT) in Basic 2, 4 and 6.

Subjects

  • English
  • Mathematics
  • Science
  • Our World, Our People
  • Religious and Moral Education
  • History
  • Creative Arts
  • Physical Education
  • Computing (B4+)
  • French (B4+)

Junior High School

JHS 1 to JHS 3

The Common Core Programme, preparing pupils for the BECE and for the academic or technical path they choose in Senior High School.

Subjects

  • English
  • Ghanaian Language
  • Mathematics
  • Science
  • Social Studies
  • Religious and Moral Education
  • Computing
  • Career Technology
  • Creative Arts and Design
  • Physical and Health Education

Assessment

How progress is measured

Class work and tests

Regular exercises, projects and end-of-term examinations, with a report card every term.

NSAT

The National Standard Assessment Test in Basic 2, 4 and 6 tracks progress against national standards.

BECE

JHS 3 pupils sit the Basic Education Certificate Examination for placement into Senior High School.
